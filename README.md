# Sales-data-dashboard

📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard
A modern and interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard designed to provide a clear overview of business performance, sales trends, product performance, regional revenue, customer contributions, and order activity.

🚀 Overview
This dashboard transforms sales data into easy-to-understand visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs). It helps users quickly monitor revenue, quantity sold, orders, customers, product performance, and regional sales.

📈 Dashboard Features
Monthly Sales Trend – Track revenue performance throughout the year.

Sales by Region – Compare sales across East, West, North, and South regions.

Profit by Region – Visualize regional profit distribution.

Top 5 Customers – Identify customers generating the highest revenue.

Sales vs Cost by Product – Compare product-level sales and costs.

Sales by Item Category – Analyze revenue across different product categories.

Quantity Sold by Product – Compare the number of units sold for each product.

Orders Over Time – Monitor monthly order volumes.

Interactive Filters – Filter data by month, region, and item category.
